# Daily Hydration Tracker 💧 https://prevailugah.github.io/drink_water/

Welcome to the **Daily Hydration Tracker**! This is a simple, interactive web application designed to help you stay healthy by tracking your daily water intake. 

The recommended daily goal is **2 Liters** (which equals about 8 glasses of water). This app makes tracking those glasses fun and easy!

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **The Visual Goal:** At the top of the screen, you'll see a large cup. This represents your total daily goal (2 Liters).
2. **The Small Cups:** Below the large cup, there are 8 smaller cups. Each small cup represents one 250ml glass of water.
3. **Tracking Your Intake:** Every time you drink a glass of water, simply click on one of the small cups! 
    - The cup will fill up (turning a vibrant purple).
    - The large cup will instantly update, showing a visual percentage of how much water you've consumed so far.
    - It will also calculate and display exactly how much water you still need to drink to reach your 2-liter goal.
4. **Making Corrections:** If you accidentally click a cup, you can click it again to "empty" it and adjust your progress. 

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Visuals:** Watch the big cup fill up with a smooth animation as you progress through your day.
- **Instant Math:** No need to calculate how much is left! The app automatically subtracts from your 2L goal and shows the remaining amount.
- **Clean & Modern UI:** A dark, sleek background with bright, contrasting colors makes it easy on the eyes and fun to use.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

This project is built using the foundational building blocks of the web:
- **HTML:** To structure the cups and text.
- **CSS:** To style the application, center elements, and provide smooth, visually pleasing animations when cups are filled.
- **JavaScript:** To add the logic—making the cups clickable, calculating the percentages, and updating the large cup dynamically.

## 🏃‍♂️ How to Run the App

There's no complex setup required! 

1. Download or clone this folder.
2. Double-click on the `index.html` file.
3. It will open directly in your default web browser, ready to use!
